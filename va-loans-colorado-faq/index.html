Frequently Asked Questions: VA Loans in Colorado

By Jerry Cusick, Mortgage Loan Officer, NMLS #649445

Last reviewed: September 21, 2026

These answers cover common VA loan questions for Colorado veterans and military families. Eligibility, entitlement and the details of a particular transaction matter, so use these answers as a starting point for a lender conversation.

Eligibility and entitlement

Who qualifies for a VA loan in Colorado?

Eligible veterans, active-duty service members, some National Guard and Reserve members, and certain surviving spouses may qualify. VA service requirements and a Certificate of Eligibility are part of the review.

Can National Guard or Reserve members qualify?

They may qualify depending on the length and type of service and the applicable VA rules. The required documents vary by service history. VA reviews qualifying active service or other eligible Guard/Reserve service paths.

Can I use a VA loan more than once?

Potentially. Ask a lender to review prior use, remaining entitlement and any restoration requirements.

Does my COE mean the loan is approved?

No. It documents VA eligibility information. The lender must still review the borrower, property and other loan requirements.

Down payment, fees and costs

Do I need a down payment?

Many eligible VA purchases do not require a down payment. Available entitlement, lender approval, the appraisal and transaction details can affect the result.

Do VA loans have PMI?

VA-backed loans do not charge monthly private mortgage insurance. A funding fee may apply unless an exemption is available.

What is the VA funding fee?

It is a one-time program fee. The applicable amount depends on the loan type and other relevant factors, including first/subsequent use and down payment where applicable.

Are all veterans exempt from the funding fee?

No. Exemption depends on VA’s qualifying categories and documentation. Confirm the status for your transaction rather than assuming it applies.

Does no down payment mean no closing costs?

No. Closing costs and other cash requirements should be reviewed separately. Ask for the full loan estimate and any applicable seller credits or financing options.

Documentation and the buying process

Do I need my COE before speaking with a lender?

No. You can begin the discussion while the certificate is being obtained. Your lender can explain which documents are needed next.

How long does a VA purchase take in Colorado?

Timing depends on documentation, appraisal, property conditions and underwriting. Discuss your target date with Jerry early so the loan steps can be coordinated with your contract and move.

What does the VA appraisal review?

It supports the value and property-requirement review for the loan. It is not a substitute for a separate home inspection.

Can I begin planning before PCS orders are final?

You can discuss your expected move and financing needs. The lender should confirm which documents and occupancy or employment details will be needed as the transaction progresses.

After closing and related benefits

Can I refinance a VA loan later?

A refinance may be available if the applicable requirements are met. The IRRRL is designed for eligible existing VA loans; other refinance options have different requirements.

Does a VA loan automatically qualify me for Colorado’s disabled-veteran property tax exemption?

No. The state tax benefit has separate eligibility, property and application rules. Check the current official Colorado instructions.
