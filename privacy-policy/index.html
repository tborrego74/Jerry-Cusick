Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains what jerrycusick.com collects, why, and what happens to it. The site is operated for Jerry Cusick, Mortgage Loan Officer, NMLS #649445, with IMB (Independent Mortgage Brokers), NMLS #2191655.

What you give us

When you send the contact form, we collect what you type into it: your name, your mobile number, where you are in the home buying process and the best time to reach you. If you choose to send your calculator estimate, the numbers you entered in the calculator are sent with it.

If you call, text or email Jerry directly, he receives whatever you share in that conversation.

What the site records on its own

The site keeps simple counts: how many visits it gets each day, the approximate city or state those visits come from, and how often the calculator is used. These are totals only. The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not store your IP address or build a profile of you.

Our hosting provider keeps standard, short-lived server logs to run and secure the site.

How your information is used

  • To respond to your request and talk with you about a home loan.
  • To prepare estimates or next steps you have asked for.
  • To keep records that mortgage regulations require.
  • To understand, in total, how the site is used so it can be improved.

We do not sell your personal information.

Calls and text messages

By sending the form you agree that Jerry Cusick may call or text you at the number you provided about your request. Consent is not a condition of any loan. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. You can also opt out by calling or emailing Jerry.

Mobile numbers and text messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

Who receives your information

  • Jerry Cusick and IMB, to serve your request.
  • Service providers that run the site and Jerry's contact systems, such as website hosting and customer relationship software. They may use your information only to provide those services.
  • Regulators, courts or law enforcement when the law requires it.

Loan applications

The online application linked from this site is a separate, secure system. Information you submit there, and information collected while your loan is processed, is covered by IMB's privacy notice, which you will receive as part of the application, and by federal and state financial privacy laws.

Keeping it safe and how long we keep it

The site uses encrypted connections. Form submissions are passed directly to Jerry's contact system and are not stored on the website itself. Contact records are kept as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal record-keeping requirements.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, account numbers or other sensitive details through the contact form, by text or by regular email.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the contact information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, at any time. Some records must be kept where the law requires it.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Links to other sites

This site links to other sites, such as the secure loan application and NMLS Consumer Access. Their privacy practices are their own.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
