DSCR Loan Requirements in Colorado: Minimum Ratio, Credit Score and Down Payment

By Jerry Cusick, Mortgage Loan Officer, NMLS #649445

Last reviewed: September 21, 2026

DSCR loan requirements vary by lender and property. A Colorado investor should confirm the qualifying rental-income calculation, credit, down payment, reserves and property rules for the specific program being considered. There is no single statewide DSCR program or universal minimum credit score. Compare written requirements for the actual loan under consideration.

Start with the lender’s calculation

Ask which rental-income evidence the lender accepts and which payment components it includes. A lease, appraisal-based market rent or permitted short-term rental income may be treated differently. The resulting ratio is a lender qualification measure, not a complete investment-profitability analysis.

Requirements by ratio profile

Ratio profileWhat the math meansWhat to discuss with your lender
1.25 or higherAt 1.25, qualifying rent is 125% of the counted payment.Ask whether the stronger ratio changes available pricing, loan-to-value or reserves. Other requirements still apply.
Around 1.0Counted rent roughly equals the counted housing payment, before other operating costs.Confirm the minimum ratio, rent documentation and cash needed for operating shortfalls.
0.75 to below 1.0Counted rent is below the counted payment.Ask whether a lower-ratio product is available at all. Do not assume this profile qualifies; more equity or reserves may be needed.

Other details to confirm

• Credit requirements and how borrower history is reviewed.

• Minimum down payment, maximum loan-to-value and reserve requirements.

• Eligible property types, unit counts and condition requirements.

• Whether an LLC or other ownership structure is accepted.

• Rental-income documentation and short-term rental treatment.

• Closing costs, pricing, prepayment terms and any other restrictions.

How to improve your position

A better-supported rent figure, more equity, stronger credit and documented reserves can improve the options available. Obtain realistic insurance and tax estimates early. Lowering an estimated payment on paper does not help if the final costs are higher. Ask Jerry to compare the same property under the available programs.

What changes for Colorado investors?

The local layer is often the property’s permitted rental use. A town’s zoning or licensing rules can affect whether a planned short-term rental operation is allowed. That does not replace lender underwriting: a property must meet both the relevant local requirements and the financing program’s rules.

Preparing a useful loan discussion

Bring the property address, intended rental use, available lease or rent information, ownership plan and the funds available for the transaction. Ask Jerry which assumptions are still estimates and which can be supported by current documents. A clear scenario makes it easier to compare actual options.

Frequently asked questions

Is there one minimum DSCR for all Colorado loans?

No. Requirements vary by lender and product. The ratio examples explain the math; they do not establish eligibility for a particular loan.

Does a larger down payment guarantee a better offer?

It may affect the available terms, but the full borrower and property review still matters. Obtain a scenario-specific comparison.

Can I qualify using projected Airbnb income?

The answer depends on local operating rules and the lender’s accepted income documentation and calculation.
