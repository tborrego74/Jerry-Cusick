Straight answers

Questions people ask before they call.

How much do I need for a down payment in Colorado?

It depends on the loan. Eligible veterans can often buy with no down payment using a VA loan. FHA loans start at 3.5% down for qualifying borrowers, and some conventional loans start at 3%. You do not need 20% down to buy a home.

What is the difference between pre-qualification and pre-approval?

A pre-qualification is an estimate based on what you tell a lender. A pre-approval means your income, assets and credit have been reviewed, so sellers and agents treat your offer as far more reliable.

What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR (debt service coverage ratio) loan is for investment property. It qualifies mainly on whether the property's rent covers its mortgage payment, instead of on your personal income documents.

What is the Good Neighbor Next Door program?

It is a HUD program that lets eligible teachers, law enforcement officers, firefighters and EMTs buy eligible HUD homes in revitalization areas at 50% off the list price, in exchange for living in the home for 36 months.

Why use a mortgage broker instead of a bank?

A bank offers its own loans. A mortgage broker can shop your loan across multiple wholesale lenders and match you to the program and pricing that fits your situation.
